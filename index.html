Who We Are

BFARMCON LIMITED is an agro-allied leader committed to sustainability and improving the agricultural value chain across Nigeria.

We are committed to bridging the gap between small-holder farming clusters and offtakers/consumers via trade finance, M&E and project management in tandem with sustainability, food safety and inclusiveness.

Samuel Joshua Funom

Managing Director • BFARMCON Limited
ABU Zaria Alumnus Industrial Design Agro Value Chain

A graduate of Ahmadu Bello University (ABU), Zaria, with a background in Industrial Design, Samuel brings over a decade of specialized hands-on expertise in agricultural field operations, value chain design, and smallholder farmer integration across Nigeria.
